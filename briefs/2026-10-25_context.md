Relevant lexicon (source `knowledge/lexicon.md`, resolved through `directed_opportunism/source`):
- Intent: “An intent is the decision to do something now (a task) in order to achieve an outcome (a purpose)” (p.110).
- Main effort: “The main effort is that single thing which will either in itself have the greatest impact or on which all other things depend. It has resourcing priority” (p.122).
- Situation: “An account of the situation, bringing out the essential features which bear on the course of action to be taken. It is useful to cover the state of knowledge, distinguishing what is known, what is probable but uncertain, and what is not known” (p.137).

- Area of responsibility: “Every unit was to have its own clearly defined area of responsibility” (p.71); here, the part of the work a role answers for.
- Decision rights: “Decision rights are appropriate if the person or group with the best knowledge and expertise in any given area is able to act in a timely manner without asking for permission” (pp.147–148). Not file or tool permissions.
