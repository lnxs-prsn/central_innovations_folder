# Capability publication edition 1

Significant change: yes. Intake and state ownership change.

Project-folder configuration, recursive innovations gathering, path-derived project identities and clone-side `.local/` writes are removed. Projects deliberately publish complete checked, hash-identified offers in their own published part. Curators read only explicitly supplied published parts, retain offers and decisions locally, and publish self-contained fixed catalogue editions. Takers ask/select from a public catalogue and keep declines and use/cost records in their own projects. UUIDs survive project moves; hashes identify fixed content and editions separately from their paths.

The contribution standard 1.0 remains the same. Complete recorded presence does not establish truth, fitness or admission. Context stays with each retained contribution; copies remain one content identity with all supplying origins. Curator decisions are effective for takers when a catalogue is published, and local declines apply immediately. Disconnected copies cannot learn later exclusions; published bytes remain directly readable.

Taking impact: use `USE.md` in order, starting with `init --project`; obtain the curator's published index through your arrangement. There is no migration of old `.local/` settings or path-scoped decisions. Preserve any historical records separately under their owner's authority; do not infer new identities or read others' old working collections. Curators follow OWNER.md with an explicit list of published parts. Missing lists perform no intake. Empty generic clones and fixed editions need no real contributions to work.

No network, background tasks, provider requirement or organization-wide command/state is introduced. Python 3.9+, standard library, portable paths; actual validation platforms are reported separately by the publisher.
