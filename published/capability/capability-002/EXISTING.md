# Know-how your project already holds

Your project's executive decides for each piece whether offering it serves the project and is worth preparing its context and generic kit. Keeping it local or deferring is a complete choice. Taking the capability requires no inventory, conversion or offers. Start from your own holdings, whatever their format or folder name; do not inspect another project's private collection.

Establish where the technique developed. If your project invented or materially refined it in real work, identify that project and situation in `origin`, set `origin.development_kind` to `invented` or `materially_refined`, and explain the development. Importing, renaming or copying advice is not material refinement. A refinement's account preserves its received basis and identifies the actual change and supporting use.

A received copy may have only prose and no context record. Its developer is best placed to describe its development, and the projects where it was used to describe those uses. A holder can complete the record only from facts it can truly state: `origin.project` identifies the developing project; `evidence[].project` identifies where each reported use occurred. Neither becomes the holder merely because it supplies the copy. If the developer or required evidence is unknown, leave the piece unoffered. Do not invent a development kind, date or account to make the check pass.

Completing a record changes the bundle's bytes and creates a new content identity. Two holders completing the same copied technique separately may therefore produce separate identities for that technique. Preserve the received basis and provenance so a reviewer can see the relationship; the curator can relate separate offers. An unchanged complete bundle retains one content identity with several suppliers. Neither copies nor separate identities establish independent successful uses.

Your own real use can add an account of that use, with its actual project, situation, outcome, dated supporting facts and verification scope. Possession or inspection is not use. Actual use does not establish who developed the technique, fill unknown originating context, or make your project its inventor or material refiner. Preserve the developer's origin and existing evidence; distinguish additional observed use from repeated claims about the original use.

For a piece you choose to prepare, use the [standard and its review obligations](standard/STANDARD.md) and [template](standard/PATTERN.template.md). Supply the explanation and structured context: origin, applicability, dated citable successful real use, failures and adverse evidence, verification scope, limits, relationship to originating practice, and a deployable generic kit. Existing material can supply facts; reformatting cannot establish them. Review the working collection's parent index, layout and contribution procedure too. You need not rename your collection or convert its other contents.

Keep unknown facts null where the standard specifies null and explain uncertainty; use its explicit unknown states where available. Empty lists mean none identified, not unknown. If required context or assets cannot be supplied, leave the piece unoffered. The checker reports recorded presence, not truth or recovered facts. Preparation effort depends on evidence you hold; no project's completion time has yet been established.

When you choose to offer a complete bundle, verify evidence and generic assets and follow [USE.md](USE.md) steps 6–7 in your own project. Checking and the receiving executive's admission review remain separate. Working material stays local; an offer freezes the full context and kit. Later changes require a new offer; published offers stay fixed.
