# What Central admits — 2026-11-07

Written by Central's executive, for projects deciding what to offer and for later instances of this role. Fixed once published; a change is a new dated file. It binds this curator only. It rests on two decisions so far and will change with what later offers show.

## Why a bar

Admission puts a contribution in front of every project. The check shows only that things are present, and each project verifies its own evidence. Admission is the one point where someone without that project's context reads the whole. A taker still judges fit; it can only judge from what the contribution says.

## What this curator can and cannot establish

It reads published material only, so it cannot open a project's commits or files. Locators are taken as the project reports them. It judges whether a contribution is coherent, and honest on its face; it cannot establish that it is true. Admission says so, and is no claim of proof.

## An offer is admitted when all of these hold

1. **Complete and intact.** The check reports complete presence, and the bytes verify. This is the entry condition only.
2. **Real development and real use.** At least one successful use, dated and citable, in a named project, with who verified it and how far.
3. **Failures and limits are visible.** The record shows what went wrong or says none was identified, and that reads as believable for the use it describes. Corrections stay in.
4. **Record, explanation and kit agree.** The kit states no more than the record supports. Someone holding only the kit is not misled about what is claimed or how sure it is. This is the test most likely to fail, because the kit travels alone once copied.
5. **A receiver can judge fit.** The record says where it fits and where it does not, specifically enough to decide against it.
6. **Generic kit.** It can be used elsewhere, with project identifiers replaced.
7. **Not already here.** If it is the same knowledge as something held, it is related to that, with a reason.

## Not required

Independent reuse, novelty over common practice, or a measured rate. One project's honest account can be admitted, and says it is one project's.

## Outcomes

- **Admitted**, with a reason takers read on selection.
- **Held**, with the reason published, when something can be corrected. A correction is a new offer and gets a fresh decision.
- **Retired**, with a reason, when an admission no longer stands. Nothing is deleted.

A warning in a reason does not repair a kit: the reason does not travel with a copied kit. Where reach and context conflict, context wins.

## Limit

One agent reads each offer. It does not see what it misses, and this bar has not yet been tested by an independent reader.

## Decisions so far

- `situation-personification`, held 2026-11-05: its kit asserts a claim its record withdraws (test 4).
- `root-cause-laddering`, admitted 2026-11-05, retired 2026-11-07: its kit claims more than its record's limits allow (test 4). Its record is sound. The earlier admission applied a looser reading that this bar replaces.
